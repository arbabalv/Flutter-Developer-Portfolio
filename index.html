Islam Dost

Millions of Muslims open 4–5 separate apps a day for prayer times, Quran, Qibla, and duas. I built one Flutter app that brings all of it together — designed, developed, and shipped solo, end-to-end.

Prayer Times

Location-based times, background Azan alarm, live countdown, monthly timetable export

Quran

Qibla Compass

Tasbeeh Counter

Duas Library

99 Names of Allah
